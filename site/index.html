Rustle

The sound of leaves. The noise the order book makes before it shows up on a candle.

Not investment advice.

Rustle is a research tool. It makes no recommendations and no performance claims, and its signals are unvalidated by design — measuring whether they have any predictive power at all is the entire point of the project. Provided “AS IS”, without warranties of any kind.

What it does

Records public Upbit trade and orderbook streams to local Parquet files, detects microstructure signals in them, and measures whether those signals predicted anything after the fact.

Method

  • Explainable rules, not a model. Every signal carries an evidence field naming what triggered it. A signal without evidence cannot be published.
  • Selection and validation are separate. Days 1–14 pick candidate rules; days 15–28 are touched only to validate them. 28 contiguous UTC days are required.
  • A rule has to clear a bar. At least 50 validation signals and a wholly-positive paired-bootstrap confidence interval.
  • No credentials, no orders. Public market data only. Simulated fills are the only “execution”, and they ignore slippage, partial fills, and fees — so paper results are an upper bound, not a forecast.

Status

Pre-validation, with a kill gate: if detected signals show no edge over a random baseline, the signal definitions change or the project stops.
